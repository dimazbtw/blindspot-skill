# Lens session L2 — Content and trust

- Lens: content_trust
- Wave: B
- Evidence tier: text
- Pages swept: home, collections, atelier, bespoke orders, visit, contact, faq
- Brief access: none
- Units: U-015

### L2-E01 | CL-01 | fail | https://brightleaf-bakery.example/
- **Check:** The purpose of the site is stated in plain words above the fold.
- **Observed:** The first heading is a slogan and the first 150 words contain no product noun (no cake, pastry or bread).
- **Verdict:** fail

### L2-E02 | CL-06 | fail | https://brightleaf-bakery.example/
- **Check:** Labels describe what the user gets or does, not the organisation's own vocabulary.
- **Observed:** "Atelier" and "Our Craft" describe the business; "Collections" is ambiguous between products and photo galleries.
- **Verdict:** fail

### L2-E03 | TR-02 | fail | https://brightleaf-bakery.example/contact
- **Check:** At least one real, direct contact route is available besides a form.
- **Observed:** The contact page and footer contain a form and social icons; no phone number, email address or postal address.
- **Verdict:** fail

### L2-E04 | CL-10 | fail | https://brightleaf-bakery.example/contact
- **Check:** Terminology is consistent.
- **Observed:** The contact form's topic list includes "Custom Cakes" and "Atelier requests" as separate options for what appears to be the same service.
- **Verdict:** fail

### L2-E05 | CT-01 | fail | https://brightleaf-bakery.example/collections
- **Check:** The page answers the questions a visitor is likely to have (price, delivery, contact, hours, area).
- **Observed:** Hours, address and delivery area are present. No page states any price, starting price or example.
- **Verdict:** fail (price only)

### L2-E06 | CT-12 | pass | https://brightleaf-bakery.example/faq
- **Check:** Help and policy copy is plain, short and answers real worries.
- **Observed:** FAQ answers use everyday words and cover cancellation notice, deposits and allergens in one or two short paragraphs each.
- **Verdict:** pass

## Lens summary

- Not assessable at this tier: image content, alt text quality and any text rendered inside images.
